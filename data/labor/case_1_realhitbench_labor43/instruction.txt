Transform the supplied labor workforce workbook into a grouped long-form Excel report. The input describes companies and their employees, with two Skill fields and two Area fields per employee. Some Company cells are merged to represent a group of consecutive employees.

The output must be one worksheet named "Labor Transformation" with these columns in order:
Company | Company Employee Count | Name | Employee Skill Count | Skill | Area

For each employee, collect the recorded nonempty Skill entries into a single vertical Skill list, and independently collect the recorded nonempty Area entries into a vertical Area list. Preserve all entries, including duplicates, spelling, case, and original order. Skill and Area are separate lists: values that appear side by side do NOT describe an asserted skill-to-area relationship. Do not create pairings, a Cartesian product, or inferred entries.

Display each employee as one contiguous group using enough rows to accommodate both lists, keeping the two lists side by side. If one list has a single value while the other requires multiple rows, show that single value as a vertically merged cell spanning the employee's group. If a list is absent, leave its cells blank. Merge the employee's Name and Employee Skill Count vertically across their group when necessary. Keep employees grouped by their original Company, merging Company and Company Employee Count over each company's whole block.

Company Employee Count is the number of employee entries belonging to that company in the ORIGINAL source, not the number of expanded output rows. Employee Skill Count is the number of nonempty original Skill entries for the employee; repeated entries count separately. These counts must be numeric cells or evaluable numeric formulas, not text. Do not normalize skill names, reorder employees, discard records, or add unrelated columns or sheets. Styling is flexible; the required Excel group merges are not optional.

Save the completed workbook as submission.xlsx.
